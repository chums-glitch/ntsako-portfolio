About Me

Who am I?

My name is Ntsako Chuma, and I'm a 21-year-old Computer Science student based in Polokwane, South Africa. Technology has always fascinated me, especially how websites and apps are built from scratch.

I started learning to code in my first year of university. Since then, I've learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, and database management. I joined the CodeTribe Bootcamp to get hands-on experience and work on real projects that prepare me for the tech industry.

What I'm working on:

Currently, I'm focused on improving my front-end skills and learning React. I'm also working on a personal project — a small website for local businesses to showcase their services online.
